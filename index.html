The Holdover

Send AB Quantum or GeoBallistics data to your board over Bluetooth. Two exports per rifle: wind at 270° and at 90°.

Bluetooth from a web page needs Chrome on Android. On iPhone use the free Bluefy browser, or on the board go to SETUP → WI-FI and scan the QR code.
